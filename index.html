Entry

Gate 7 is the best entrance for suite access. Check your ticket for your recommended gate before the Climb to Kickoff at 5:55 PM. Guests are welcome to arrive any time gates are open. About 60 minutes before kickoff is a comfortable window to beat the crowd and settle into the suite before the game begins.

Rideshare

Arrival
If you're arriving via Rideshare, you'll be dropped off at the northeast corner of the stadium near Gate 5.

Departure
All rideshare pickups are at the Ball Arena Rideshare Lot on 5th & Walnut. This is about a 15-minute walk from the stadium.

Lightrail & Bus

The C, E, and W lines stop at Empower Field at Mile High Station, about a 15-minute walk from Gate 8.

Buy your return fare before the game to skip the postgame line.
