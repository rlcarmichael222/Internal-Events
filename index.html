Bill Ritter
Freestone Strategies · Partner

Background

Former Governor of Colorado (Democrat), 2007 to 2011. He was Colorado's 41st governor.

Earlier served as Denver District Attorney for about 12 years and practiced law at Hogan & Hartson before running for governor.

Founded the Center for the New Energy Economy at Colorado State University in 2011 and directed it until his recent retirement.

Current role and organization

Joined Freestone Strategies as a partner (announced February 2025). The firm was founded in 2016 and provides public affairs, media and crisis communications, and government relations, with a focus on energy and environment.

Relevance

Strong Colorado government and energy-policy relationships from his time as governor and at CSU. Useful for understanding how clean energy policy has developed in Colorado.

Naomi Colwell
Aurora Chamber of Commerce · President & CEO

Background

Joined the Aurora Chamber in 1999. Served as marketing and events director, then left in 2017 to become president of the Brighton Chamber of Commerce.

Returned to Aurora as interim president and CEO on July 1, 2023, when Kevin Hougen retired after leading the Chamber since 1999. Now serves as President/CEO.

More than 20 years of experience across metro-area chambers, including event management, finance, sales, and sponsorships.

Current role and organization

Other roles: Executive Director, Aurora Police Foundation; board member, Open Space Advisory Board, City of Centennial; has worked with Cycle of Hope, an organization serving people with disabilities.

Relevance

Leads the chamber for a major Aurora business community in the east metro area, including Leadership Aurora programming.

Kelly Flenniken
Colorado Counties, Inc. (CCI), Denver · Executive Director

Background

First woman to lead CCI, effective October 16, 2023. Succeeded John Swartout, who retired.

Previously director of community relations at Xcel Energy. Former executive director of the Grand Junction Economic Partnership.

Has held local government positions in Denver and Chicago. Brings nearly 20 years of experience in public policy, leadership, and government relations.

Current role and organization

CCI is a nonprofit membership association founded in 1907 that supports county commissioners, mayors, and council members. It advocates for counties before the General Assembly and runs the CCI Foundation, which hosts statewide conferences. CCI's website now says it represents all 64 counties; 2023 coverage said 62 of 64.

Relevance

Key contact for county-level policy in Colorado, including land use, housing affordability, and county government administration. Has an energy-industry background through Xcel Energy.
